Nexo

A private chat app with end-to-end encrypted messages.

Download for Android
  1. Tap Download for Android on your phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser.
  3. If Play Protect warns you, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  4. Open Nexo and create an account with your email.

Android only. The warning appears because Nexo isn’t on the Play Store yet.
